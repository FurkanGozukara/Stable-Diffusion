MiniMax H3 Image makes 16 megapixel pictures in only 6 steps on your own NVIDIA GPU, and it now has a second text to image preset for small objects: "MiniMax H3 Image - Text To Image 16 MP - KJNodes Empty Image - 6 Steps - 261114". It ships ready beside the standard preset in our SECourses ComfyUI installer V152 and in Amazing SwarmUI Presets v90, and in SwarmUI it is one checkbox of our MiniMaxH3Image extension. On many images it draws small objects more correctly: gem settings, watch gears, tools, bike sprockets and plated dishes. The model, 16 MP, 6 steps and MiniMax's official prompt enhancer running locally stay the same. Faces and rooms often look glossier and more plastic with it, so the standard preset stays the choice for portraits and natural skin.

You will learn which preset fits which picture, from same-caption, same-seed pairs: each comparison shows the whole 16 MP picture first and then zooms into the small part where the two presets differ. In ComfyUI you open the new preset from the Presets folder with Ctrl+O, see that the prompt box, output size, prompt enhancer and seed number match the standard preset, and find the one difference: the MiniMax H3 Prompt Weight node of KJNodes in "text with empty image" mode, which shows the model an empty black picture beside your text for the first quarter of the steps (2 of 6); the other 4 steps run on your text alone. Then you type a short idea, keep the enhancer on, click Run and look closely at the watch movement of the 16 MP result. In SwarmUI you filter the Presets tab for H3 Image, reset the parameters in Quick Tools, Direct Apply the new card and find its KJNodes Empty Image checkbox, which also works with your own settings. Finally you generate a jeweler at 16 MP and zoom into the ring.

Which preset when: the new preset when small objects carry the picture (watch movements, rings and gem settings, tools, sprockets, plated dishes); the standard preset for portraits, natural skin and interiors that should look like a photograph. For an important image, run both with the same prompt and keep the better one. The new preset is text to image only; for photo edits keep the Image Edit preset. No model yet? The MiniMax-H3 Core Bundle in the Model Downloader fetches every file; the Low VRAM Bundle needs less GPU memory.
