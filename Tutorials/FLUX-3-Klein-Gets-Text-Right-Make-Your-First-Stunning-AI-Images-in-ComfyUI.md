ComfyUI runs FLUX 3 Klein on your own NVIDIA computer, and our advanced text-to-image preset gathers prompt, output size, seed and result into numbered groups. In part 2 of our ComfyUI mini series you start the fresh installation and make three images: a 2720 x 1536 aurora conservatory, a 4-megapixel sapphire kingfisher and a Night Bloom poster with its words exactly as written. Then we test our HQ INT8 ConvRot model: up to 2.3x faster warm transformer steps than the original BF16 on the same RTX 3090 (loading, text encoding and image decoding excluded), a transformer file about 45 percent smaller, and a live generation on an RTX 5090.
