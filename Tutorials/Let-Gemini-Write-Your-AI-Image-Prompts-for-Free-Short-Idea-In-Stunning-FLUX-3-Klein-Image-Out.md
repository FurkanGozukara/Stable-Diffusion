Our FLUX 3 Klein prompt guide, included in the ComfyUI package, teaches a language model to turn a short idea into one complete scene caption. In part 3 of our ComfyUI mini series we use it in Google AI Studio with Gemini 3.8 Flash, which currently has a free tier for this kind of text work, and then render the caption in ComfyUI with our advanced text-to-image preset: a glass conservatory beneath an aurora at a wide 2720 x 1536.
