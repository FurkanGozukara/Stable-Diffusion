ComfyUI with our advanced FLUX 3 Klein image edit preset changes what your caption describes and keeps what it names. In part 4 of our ComfyUI mini series a celadon vase becomes cobalt and gold with its dragon handles kept, a desk loses its plant while the pen moves and a croissant replaces the cup, a still-life painting becomes a bright food photograph, and a white cat with one amber and one blue eye moves into a brass submarine and a woodblock print. The preset shares the gallery, resolution and seed controls of our text-to-image workflow, and our gallery keeps the reference order visible.
