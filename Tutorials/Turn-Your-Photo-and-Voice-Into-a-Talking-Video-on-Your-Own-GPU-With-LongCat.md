Our new LongCat Avatar 1.6 "3 Steps Really Fast" preset turns one photo and one audio file into a lip-synced talking video 2.9 times faster than the default 8 steps, with nearly the same picture: 4 min 24 s instead of 12 min 35 s for the same 15-second 720p clip on one RTX 5090 (conditions below). We built this 3-step pipeline ourselves; it ships for ComfyUI (our installer, V154) and SwarmUI (presets pack v92), and one click in our SwarmUI Model Downloader (v195) gets every model. You also see MiniMax H3 break on 30 seconds of audio while LongCat keeps going, up to a two-minute lesson made in one run.

Tested November 19, 2026 on one RTX 5090 32 GB (ComfyUI V154 with --reserve-vram 3, presets as shipped, same photo, 15.36 s audio and seed, 800 x 1152, one job at a time): 8 steps 12 min 35 s, 4 steps 6 min 33 s, 3 steps 4 min 24 s with the models loaded (2.9 times faster). Our 3-step times apply that day's LongCat node update at 8.2 percent faster, its smaller measured gain. AI demo clips are shown upscaled 4x with RelayVSR (SECourses Premium Upscaler); times are generation times. SSIM against 8 steps: 0.87 (3 steps), 0.89 (4 steps). 3 steps: 31.5 s of audio in 9 min 23 s, 60 s in 17 min 9 s, 119.6 s in 34 min 24 s. MiniMax H3 Lip Synch Turbo 4 Steps at 832 x 1248: 15 s in 4 min 27 s; the 31.5 s chef audio ran 12 min 20 s and returned grey noise. The barista run in the video took about five minutes with the screen recording running.

Our pipeline: one premerged model with an exact in-place switch between the DMD and OPSD merges (no LoRA work at run time), three of the official eight distilled steps, INT8 attention and a last segment only as long as the audio. 3.7-second segments continue each other with your photo as the identity anchor, and the saved video keeps your audio.

This video is for creators, marketers, teachers and small businesses who want adverts, lessons and presenter clips from a photo and a voice on their own NVIDIA GPU. New to ComfyUI or SwarmUI? Start with the tutorials above. Thank you for watching.
