You will learn installation, models, captions, image edits, layouts, batches, shared storage, launch arguments and updates. Follow the complete route from the download post to a working image. Open saved workflows, place references, inspect enlarged results and compare changes using sliders and labeled boards. The examples show exact lettering, object preservation and ten-reference composition.

Main topics include HQ INT8 ConvRot, Gemini, seeds, LoRAs and VRAM. Benchmarks: up to 2.3x on RTX 3090 and 1.82–2.18x on RTX 5090, for warm transformer steps only; loading, text encoding and image decoding excluded. Learn what --reserve-vram changes, why a saved BAT needs a server restart, and how to verify a small workflow afterward. Change one option at a time and compare the same task.

Use the chapters to jump to each setup step, feature and comparison.
