You will learn how to use Draw, Grab, Smooth and Clay deliberately; adjust brush radius and strength; protect a region with masks; and separate primary form, broad planes and small detail. Shape the terrain around a useful docking footprint, build two different rock silhouettes, and inspect ground contact and camera composition. Compare voxel remeshing, dynamic topology and Multiresolution on separate studies so you can choose between rebuilding a surface, adding local density and preserving a subdivision hierarchy. Keep a simpler checkpoint before committing to extra geometry.

Main topics include mask inversion, hidden geometry and face sets; an aligned low/high rock pair for later baking; Add Primitives in Sculpt Mode; Scene Project; color attributes during remeshing; Shrinkwrap-assisted quad placement; face orientation; and material roughness. Build an independent coarse male head and a neutral eyelid strip for a later motion study. Recorded with Blender 5.2.2 LTS.

Use the chapters to jump to a tool or comparison, then open the matching checkpoint to continue from that state.
