MiniMax H3 Image is a new image model that creates and edits pictures at 16 megapixels (4096 × 4096 or a matching tall or wide size) in only 6 steps on your own NVIDIA GPU, with no upscaler. It now runs in SwarmUI with our two new presets in Amazing SwarmUI Presets V89, Text To Image and Image Edit with up to 16 images, every model and setting already chosen. Our MiniMaxH3Image extension runs MiniMax's own prompt enhancer locally: a few words in any language become the long, detailed description the model was trained on. In this tutorial a weathered house is renovated with one short sentence, one typed line becomes a 16 MP ice cream ad with every letter right, and you see a bedroom staged from 4 photos and a pizza photo turned into a poster.

You will learn how to find both presets by typing 261111 in the Presets tab, reset the parameters and apply the Text To Image preset with Direct Apply. Then you write a short idea in any language, put every word that must appear on the picture in quotes, keep 16 megapixels and the reasoning enhancer, use fast mode for quick tries or off for your own long prompts, and read the enhancer's full description in the metadata. With the Image Edit preset you add your photo as image 1, write the change as one plain sentence with no mask, and name more pictures with @image2, @image3 and so on. Finally you get the latest zip from our SwarmUI post and open the MiniMax-H3 Core Bundle in the Model Downloader: the model (about 20 GB), its VAE (1 GB), the prompt enhancer (9 GB) and the text encoder (15 GB); the Low VRAM Bundle needs less GPU memory.
