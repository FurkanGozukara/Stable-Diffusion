Edit real videos with MiniMax H3 in ComfyUI and SwarmUI, and let the presets do the work. Give Background Replace your video and one image: there is no prompt to write, the person stays exactly as filmed and everything around them becomes the new place. The same release relights a whole clip, puts a new face on someone, drives a car along a path you draw on a photo, upscales real footage, turns a man on a New York street into a knight while the traffic keeps moving, and makes a 20-second video that follows five scenes instead of repeating. Every clip in this showcase comes from a real run of the shipped presets.

You will see every new preset in use, with on-screen steps. Background Replace needs only your video and a background image: SAM 3.1 finds the person, their original pixels are kept and pasted back, and the rest is made new from your image, moving with your camera. Video Relight writes a complete lighting plan for H3 from one of 20 looks, or from up to three lights you design. Face Swap puts a new face on the person in your clip. Cut And Drag shows exactly how it works: your photo, the path you draw, the rough drag, and the real video Pika H3 Flex makes from it, with the subject and the prompt found for you. Video Upscale takes real footage reduced to 720p and brings it to 2560 pixels, shown with a slider in the same frame. Masked Edit replaces one person or object and keeps every other pixel as filmed. Long Video gives each part of the video its own scene with --- lines in the prompt. Our live token counter shows what a run needs before you press Run, moving from green to amber to red as you reach the limit.

Main topics include MiniMax H3, automatic background replacement without a green screen, MiniMax H3 Genjutsu masked video editing, AI face swap for video, video relighting, Pika H3 Flex, cut and drag motion control, real video upscaling with the H3 latent upscaler, long AI video with per-window scenes, automatic prompts from a local vision model, ComfyUI presets, SwarmUI presets and the one-click MiniMax-H3 Core Bundle of our model downloader. The upscaler redraws fine detail, so small text and fine logos can change; landscapes, animals and textures gain the most. Background Replace keeps people of similar size and lets distant passers-by go, and it tells H3 to lock the camera when your clip's camera does not move, so the new place stays steady; we tested it on a 31-second clip of 736 frames. Long clips work the same way as short ones, because the video is made in overlapping windows, and the token counter then reads per window. Cut And Drag finds the subject and writes the prompt when you leave them empty, and First Last Frame and References To Video write their own prompt too. Run a Background Replace, Masked Edit or Relight result through Video Upscale for a sharper final video. Typed prompts are always used exactly as written, and all of it keeps what our MiniMax H3 presets already offer: folder batches, the optional LoRA stack and low VRAM options.
