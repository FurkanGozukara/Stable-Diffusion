MiniMax H3 Image is a new 33.1B image model that generates and edits pictures at 16 megapixels, 4096 × 4096 or a matching wide or tall size, in only 6 sampling steps on your own NVIDIA GPU, with no upscaler. Our SECourses ComfyUI installer V150 ships two ready presets for it, Text To Image 16 MP and Image Edit Up To 16 Images, with every model and setting already chosen. MiniMax's official prompt enhancer runs locally inside them in three modes (reasoning, fast and off): it turns a short idea in any language into the detailed caption the model was trained on, picks the aspect ratio, and a box shows the exact prompt the model received. The SECourses Model Downloader V190 fetches every model file into the right folders from one bundle. In this mini tutorial a rusted, moss-covered Volkswagen Beetle is restored with one plain sentence, 9 typed words become a 16 MP photo of a fox, and you see a room furnished from 4 photos, letter-perfect posters and 1 MP vs 4 MP vs 16 MP.

You will learn how to open the MiniMax H3 Image text to image preset from the Presets folder with Ctrl+O, write a short idea in box 1 in any language, keep the recommended 16 MP output size, use the prompt enhancer in reasoning mode for the best captions or fast mode for quick tries, switch it off when you write a long caption yourself, read the exact prompt the model received, and inspect the result at full size. In the image edit preset you add your photo to the gallery as @image1, write the change as one plain sentence with no mask, say what must stay the same (the forest, the light, the camera angle), and name more reference images with @image2 and @image3. Finally you set up the route yourself: download the ComfyUI V150 zip from our Patreon post, extract it over your ComfyUI folder, run the update file, install bundle 100 with the bundles installer, then open the MiniMax-H3 Core Bundle in the Model Downloader (4 files for images, about 44 GB) or the Low VRAM Bundle, which brings a smaller version of the image model.

Measured numbers and their conditions: on an RTX 5090 with the models loaded, 6 steps, prompt encoding and decoding included and the prompt enhancer not included, 1 MP took 3.4 s, 4 MP 5.7 s and 16 MP 15.7 s. With ComfyUI limited to 12 GB of the same card's memory, a 16 MP image still finished in 19.5 s, the rest streaming from system RAM; a real 12 GB card computes slower. On camera, the fast enhancer mode made a second 16 MP fox in under half a minute from the click, and reasoning mode adds about a minute for the best captions.

This video is for ComfyUI users and AI image makers with an NVIDIA GPU who want the newest local image model at its full 16 megapixel quality without collecting files, nodes and settings by hand: open a preset, write one sentence and click Run. If ComfyUI is new for you, start with the installation tutorial linked above; if it is already installed, the update tutorial shows the zip update step by step.
