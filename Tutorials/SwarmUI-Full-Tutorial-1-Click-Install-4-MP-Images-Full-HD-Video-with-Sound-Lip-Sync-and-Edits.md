SwarmUI, set up with our one-click installers, our model downloader (35+ bundles, 517+ models) and 86+ ready presets, turns your own PC into a complete AI studio: 4-megapixel images, one-prompt edits, full HD MiniMax H3 videos with speech, sound effects and music, lip sync and Genjutsu person swaps. This tutorial installs everything from zero on Windows and teaches every feature step by step; the same installers run on RunPod, SimplePod, Massed Compute and Linux.
