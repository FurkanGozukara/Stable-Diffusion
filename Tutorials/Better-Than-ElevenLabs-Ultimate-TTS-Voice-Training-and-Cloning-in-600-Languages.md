Train your own AI voice with Ultimate TTS. This complete Windows tutorial covers voice cloning, automatic take selection, multilingual speech and OmniVoice full fine-tuning. The app also supports RunPod, SimplePod, Massed Compute and local Linux.

Links:

Download Ultimate TTS and read the source post: [ [https://www.patreon.com/SECourses/posts/ultimate-text-to-139297407](https://www.patreon.com/SECourses/posts/ultimate-text-to-139297407) ]

Windows Requirements tutorial: [ [https://youtu.be/DrhUHnYfwC0](https://youtu.be/DrhUHnYfwC0) ]

You will learn how to install the app, clone an included voice, prepare references, compare takes, control pronunciation and timing, export subtitles and MP4s, run batches, audit a dataset and deploy a newly trained voice.

Main topics include OmniVoice, IndexTTS 2.5, AuK, 9 language examples, BF16 and ConvRot INT8, speaker similarity, Whisper checks, Full/LoRA/DoRA training, checkpoint grids and voice presets.

Use the chapters to jump directly to each control, example and training step.
